Clients and individuals may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data. We will respond to verified data requests within 30 days.

🛡️ Compliance Posture

Texas AI Automation is committed to maintaining a strong security and compliance posture. Our current practices are aligned with the following frameworks:

✓ NIST AI Risk Management Framework — trustworthy AI deployment practices
✓ NIST Privacy Framework — data minimization and local processing principles
✓ SOC 2 principles — security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy controls
✓ GDPR & CCPA/CPRA alignment — data minimization, purpose limitation, and individual rights

We are transparent about our current state: we follow these frameworks in practice and are working toward formal SOC 2 Type II certification. We do not claim certifications we have not yet achieved.

⚖️ Law Enforcement
