Admin & Security Contact

Security disclosures, abuse reporting, data requests, and compliance inquiries.

Responsible Disclosure

If you discover a security vulnerability in our systems, please report it responsibly. We take all security reports seriously and will respond within 48 hours.

Email: admin@texaskrystal.com
Subject line: SECURITY DISCLOSURE

Please include a description of the vulnerability, steps to reproduce, and potential impact. We request that you do not publicly disclose the issue until we have had an opportunity to investigate and address it.

Abuse Reporting

To report misuse of our services, unauthorized monitoring, or any concerns about how our technology is being deployed, contact us immediately.

Email: admin@texaskrystal.com
Subject line: ABUSE REPORT
Phone: 832-224-5918

Data Requests

Clients and individuals may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data. We will respond to verified data requests within 30 days.

Email: admin@texaskrystal.com
Subject line: DATA REQUEST

Please include: your name, the nature of your request (access, correction, deletion, or export), and information sufficient for us to verify your identity and locate the relevant data.

Frameworks We Reference

These frameworks guide our security and privacy practices:

✓ NIST AI Risk Management Framework — trustworthy AI deployment practices
✓ NIST Privacy Framework — data minimization principles
✓ GDPR & CCPA/CPRA — data minimization, purpose limitation, and individual rights

These frameworks are references, not certifications, and we do not promise compliance.
