From single-location food trucks to multi-site operators. Examples on this page show what can be configured. We confirm what applies to your business during your audit.

Fuel & Convenience Stores

Gas stations and c-stores handle thousands of customer interactions daily. Most insights — product requests, complaints, safety issues — are lost because no one has time to track them manually.

With recording notice and appropriate consent, our AI can analyze counter and drive-thru conversations, log stockout mentions and product requests, and flag potential register exceptions for human review. Owners receive a nightly digest of what happened, what customers want, and what needs attention.

Conversation analysis requires recording notice and appropriate consent. Access to recordings and transcripts is restricted, retention is set during configuration, and alerts flag potential issues for human review.

Example Automations

Customer request logging → auto-populated reorder sheet
Possible escalations → alert to a manager for human review
Potential register exceptions → audio and POS matching for human review
Shelf inventory counts → photo-based stock tracking
Nightly summary → emailed report of the full shift

Restaurants & Quick Service

Restaurant operations lose money in hard-to-see ways: an order isn't rung up, equipment breaks and nobody reports it, or high-demand items run out without the manager knowing.

Our AI compares spoken orders with register activity to flag possible missed ring-ups for human review. Equipment issues mentioned in conversation can create maintenance requests. Photo-based kitchen inventory reduces manual counting and supports prep planning.

Conversation analysis requires recording notice and appropriate consent. Access to recordings and transcripts is restricted, retention is set during configuration, and alerts flag potential issues for human review.

Example Automations

Order-to-register matching → flag possible missed ring-ups
Void/refund patterns → flag for human review
Equipment issue mentions → maintenance requests
Kitchen inventory photos → daily prep forecasting
Vendor invoice processing → straight to accounting

Bars & Hospitality

Beverage shrink from overpouring, unrecorded comps, and undocumented waste silently drains margins. Manual bottle counting is tedious and error-prone.

Photo-based pour tracking lets staff snap end-of-shift bottle photos. The system counts fill levels, syncs with POS sales data, and estimates variance costs, flagging differences for review and generating reorder signals when stock hits thresholds.

Example Automations

Photo-based bottle count → real vs. theoretical usage
POS-synced variance analysis → cost per pour
Possible overpouring and spillage flagged for review
Automated reorder signals at threshold
