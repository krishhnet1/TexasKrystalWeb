Industries We Serve

Industry-agnostic AI.
Industry-specific outcomes.

From single-location food trucks to multi-site enterprise operators — our frameworks scale to fit your operation.

⛽

Fuel & Convenience Stores

Gas stations and c-stores handle thousands of customer interactions daily. Most insights — product requests, complaints, safety issues — are lost because no one has time to track them manually.

Our AI listens to counter and drive-thru conversations, detects stockout mentions, logs product requests customers are asking for, monitors cash handling at registers, and flags conflict or compliance issues in real time. Owners receive a nightly digest of what happened, what customers want, and what needs attention.

Key Automations

📋 Customer request logging → auto-populated reorder sheet
🚨 Escalation alerts → SMS to manager within seconds
💰 Register fraud detection → audio-visual transaction matching
📸 Shelf inventory counts → photo-based stock tracking
📊 Nightly summary → emailed report of the full shift

Key Automations

🎙️ Order-to-register matching → catch missed ring-ups
🔍 Void/refund pattern detection → flag suspicious trends
🔧 Equipment issue mentions → automatic maintenance tickets
📸 Kitchen inventory photos → daily prep forecasting
📄 Vendor invoice processing → straight to accounting
🍔

Restaurants & Quick Service

Restaurant operations lose money in invisible ways — a cashier pockets cash, an order isn't rung up, equipment breaks and nobody reports it, high-demand items run out without the manager knowing.

Our audio-visual AI correlates spoken orders with register activity to detect sweethearting and missed ring-ups. Equipment issues mentioned in conversation auto-generate maintenance requests. Photo-based kitchen inventory eliminates hours of manual counting and powers prep forecasting.

🏪

Retail & Multi-Location

Multi-location retailers need consistent operational intelligence across all stores without deploying an army of district managers.

Our AI monitors customer sentiment and product demand signals across locations, automates shelf inventory counts from photos, detects shrink patterns, and delivers cross-location comparison reports nightly. See which store is performing, which needs attention, and what customers are asking for everywhere — in one dashboard.

Key Automations

📈 Cross-location product demand aggregation
📸 Automated shelf-gap detection from photos
🛡️ Shrink and theft pattern recognition
😊 Customer sentiment tracking per location
📊 Nightly performance comparison reports

Key Automations

🍷 Photo-based bottle count → real vs. theoretical usage
💲 POS-synced variance analysis → cost per pour
🚨 Overpouring and spillage detection
📦 Automated reorder signals at threshold
🍷

Bars & Hospitality

Beverage shrink from overpouring, unrecorded comps, and undocumented waste silently drains margins. Manual bottle counting is tedious and error-prone.

Photo-based pour tracking lets staff snap end-of-shift bottle photos. The system counts fill levels, syncs with POS sales data, and calculates exact variance costs — pinpointing where product is disappearing and generating automatic reorder signals when stock hits thresholds.

📊

Accounting & Back Office

Automate the mountain of invoices, receipts, and spreadsheets that consume hours of manual work every week. Our IDP pipeline extracts, validates, and exports — all locally.

Ideal for multi-vendor operations managing five or more portal subscriptions with overlapping invoice formats, date ranges, and pricing structures.

🚚

Food Trucks & Mobile Vendors

Lightweight edge deployment that fits mobile operations. Track daily sales conversations from a single microphone, manage inventory from phone photos, and receive end-of-day reports via text.

No server room required — runs on a compact edge device that travels with your truck.
