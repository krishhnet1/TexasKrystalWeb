What We Automate

Three pillars of
operational intelligence

We process conversations, documents, and images on local infrastructure — so your sensitive business data never touches a public cloud.

🎙️

Conversation Intelligence

Audio from counters, drive-thrus, and phones is transcribed and analyzed in real time. Detect customer requests, complaints, out-of-stock mentions, equipment issues, and escalation triggers — all converted into structured reports and instant alerts.

📄

Document & Invoice Automation

PDFs, invoices, receipts, and spreadsheets are processed locally using intelligent document recognition. Extract fields, route approvals, match purchase orders, and export clean data to QuickBooks, Xero, or Excel — zero manual entry.

📸

Photo-to-Inventory Counts

Snap a photo of shelves, coolers, or back-of-house storage. Our vision AI identifies and counts every item — cigarette packs, beverage bottles, produce bins — and generates reorder signals. No more manual counting.
