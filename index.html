What we automate

Three AI automation services, built to run on local infrastructure at your business.

Conversation Intelligence

Audio from counters, drive-thrus, and phones is transcribed and analyzed in real time, with posted notice and employee consent. Detect customer requests, complaints, out-of-stock mentions, equipment issues, and escalation triggers.

Document & Invoice Automation

PDFs, invoices, receipts, and spreadsheets are processed locally. Extract fields, route approvals, match purchase orders, and export clean data to QuickBooks, Xero, or Excel.

Photo-to-Inventory Counts

Snap a photo of shelves, coolers, or back-of-house storage. Vision AI identifies and counts items such as cigarette packs, beverage bottles, and produce bins, then generates reorder signals.

Capture, process, act

Every automation follows the same five steps.

  1. 1
    Capture
    Audio, documents, images, or spreadsheets enter the pipeline from your existing systems.
  2. 2
    Transcribe & extract
    Local AI models convert raw inputs into structured text, data fields, and item counts.
  3. 3
    Classify & detect
    Models running on your hardware identify events, exceptions, requests, and anomalies.
  4. 4
    Alert & trigger
    SMS, email, or dashboard alerts for escalations, theft signals, and manager requests.
  5. 5
    Report
    Every night you receive a summary of what customers asked for, what's broken, and what to order next.
