AI automation that
works while you sleep

Three core automation services. Examples on this page show what can be configured. We confirm what applies to your business during your audit.

Conversation
Intelligence

Audio from your point of sale, drive-thru, counter, or phone lines can be captured, transcribed, and analyzed by AI models, with recording notice and appropriate consent. The system flags operational events for review.

Configured alerts and a shift summary give the owner or manager a place to review what was flagged.

Conversation analysis requires recording notice and appropriate consent. Access to recordings and transcripts is restricted, retention is set during configuration, and alerts flag potential issues for human review.

Examples of What Can Be Flagged

→ Customer requests — products asked for but not in stock, logged with timestamp and frequency
→ Complaints & issues — broken equipment, dirty restrooms, wrong orders, service complaints
→ Possible escalations — manager requests or possible conflict → alert for human review. This is not an emergency detection service.
→ Potential transaction exceptions — possible missed ring-ups, voids, or register discrepancies for human review
→ Nightly digest — a structured report of the day's events, trends, and recommended actions

Document & Invoice
Automation

Intelligent Document Processing (IDP) combines OCR with machine learning to handle invoices, receipts, purchase orders, and any PDF-based paperwork. Fields are extracted, validated, matched, and routed, with exceptions sent for human review.

Export clean data directly to QuickBooks, Xero, Excel, or your ERP. We agree where information is processed, who can access it, and which external services receive it before deployment.

Example Workflow

1
Invoice scanned, uploaded, or received via email
2
OCR + AI extracts vendor, amounts, dates, line items
3
Validation against PO data, duplicate detection, anomaly flags
4
Straight-through export to accounting software or human review queue

Photo-to-Inventory
Vision AI

Take a photo of any shelf, cooler, freezer, or storage area. Our computer vision models identify items, count quantities, and flag differences from expected stock levels for review.

Reduce manual counting. Staff snap photos during their shift, and the system helps with identification, counting, and reorder signals. Results can sync with supported POS systems for variance analysis.

Examples of What Can Be Counted

Cigarette packs & cartons
Beverage bottles & cans
Shelf products & snacks
Bar bottles & pour levels
Produce & perishables
Back-of-house storage
