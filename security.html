Conversation analysis

Conversation analysis involves recording people at work and in stores, so notice, consent, access and retention are set before deployment.

What We Set Up With You

✓ Recording notice where audio is captured, such as a counter, phone line, or drive-thru
✓ Appropriate consent from the people recorded, including employee acknowledgements and clear policies
✓ Access restrictions and retention — who can see recordings and transcripts, how long they are kept, and whether raw audio is kept at all
✓ No biometric identification — we analyze conversations for operational events, not voice identity profiles
✓ Human review — alerts flag potential issues for people to review. This is not an emergency detection service, it does not decide that theft occurred, and SMS delivery is not guaranteed

Recording and consent rules vary by location and situation, and they may apply differently to conversations you are not part of. Please consult legal counsel for your specific use.
